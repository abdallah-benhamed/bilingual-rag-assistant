How it works

  1. Your question is matched against the company's documents with hybrid search (keywords and Gemini embeddings), in the language you wrote in.
  2. If nothing relevant is found, the assistant does not call the model: it offers a person instead.
  3. Gemini answers only from the matching sections and must cite them; an answer without a valid citation is discarded.
  4. Every message, source and handoff is logged in the admin.
