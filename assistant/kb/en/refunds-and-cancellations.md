# Refunds and cancellations

## Cancelling an order
Cancelling is free before the parcel is picked up. After pickup, the delivery fee is charged.

## Late deliveries
If a same-city delivery is more than 60 minutes late, we refund the delivery fee automatically as app credit within 24 hours.

## Damaged or missing items
Report the problem in the app within 48 hours, with photos. After review, we refund the amount to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Refund methods
Card payments are refunded to the same card; the bank takes 5 to 10 working days. Cash payments are refunded as app credit or by bank transfer within 5 working days.
