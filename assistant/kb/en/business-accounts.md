# Business accounts

## Who can open a business account
Shops and restaurants located in our delivery zones can open a business account if they have a tax ID (matricule fiscal).

## Business prices
Businesses pay 4 TND per same-city delivery, invoiced monthly. Above 300 deliveries a month, the price drops to 3.5 TND per delivery.

## Cash collection for businesses
Couriers collect cash from your customers on delivery. We transfer the money to your bank account every Tuesday and Friday, with a 1% fee.

## Getting started as a business
Fill in the "Business account" form in the app. An account manager calls you within 1 working day.
