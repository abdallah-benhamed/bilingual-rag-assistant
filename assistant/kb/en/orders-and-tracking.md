# Orders and tracking

## Placing an order
You can order from the Nakhla app on Android and iPhone, or from our website. You can pay cash on delivery or by bank card in the app.

## Tracking an order
After you order, you receive an SMS with a tracking link. An order goes through four statuses: Received, Picked up, On the way, Delivered.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the address in the app while the order status is Received. Once the parcel is picked up, call customer service; there is an extra 3 TND fee if the courier has to change route.

## Parcel limits
A parcel can weigh up to 20 kg and measure up to 60 x 40 x 40 cm. We do not carry cash, prescription medicines or live animals.
