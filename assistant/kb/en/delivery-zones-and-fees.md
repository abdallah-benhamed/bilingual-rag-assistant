# Delivery zones and fees

## Zones we cover
Nakhla Delivery currently delivers in Zarzis, Djerba (Houmt Souk and Midoun), Medenine city and Ben Gardane. We do not deliver anywhere outside these zones yet.

## Delivery fees
Delivery costs 5 TND within the same city and 9 TND between two cities in our zones, for example from Zarzis to Djerba. Delivery is free within the same city for orders above 80 TND.

## Delivery times
Within the same city, orders placed before 18:00 are delivered within 2 hours. Deliveries between cities arrive the next day before 13:00.

## Opening hours
We work Monday to Saturday from 8:00 to 21:00. On Sunday we work from 10:00 to 18:00, for same-city deliveries only.
